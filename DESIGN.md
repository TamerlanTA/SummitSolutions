# Design System: PromAlp Almaty

## Colors (OKLCH)
- **Background**: `oklch(15% 0.01 250)` (Deep tinted navy/ink)
- **Background Elevated**: `oklch(20% 0.015 250)`
- **Ink (Text)**: `oklch(95% 0.005 250)` (Off-white)
- **Ink Muted**: `oklch(70% 0.01 250)`
- **Accent (Cyan)**: `oklch(75% 0.15 190)` (Technical cyan/electric)
- **Accent (Safety Orange)**: `oklch(65% 0.2 45)` (High-visibility warning)
- **Border**: `oklch(30% 0.02 250)`

## Typography
- **Headings**: Inter, tight tracking, bold weights.
- **Technical/Mono**: JetBrains Mono for stats, HUD elements, and labels.
- **Body**: Inter, 400-500 weight, 1.6 line height.

## Elevation
- No soft shadows. Use `ring` borders and slight background shifts.
- 1px borders with subtle gradients for "glass" effects where appropriate, but kept minimal.

## Components
- **Buttons**: Square or slightly rounded (4px). High contrast.
- **Sections**: Clear dividers, often using grid lines or "blueprint" style backgrounds.
- **HUD Elements**: Data overlays with thin borders and mono text.
