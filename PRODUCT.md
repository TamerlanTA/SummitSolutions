# Product: PromAlp Almaty

## Users
- Property Managers of high-end residential complexes (LCD).
- Owners and managers of commercial office buildings.
- Industrial facility maintenance directors.
- Government and municipal infrastructure planners.

## Brand
- **Core Identity**: High-precision engineering meets extreme logistics. We are the "Special Ops" of building maintenance.
- **Visual Language**: HUD-inspired, technical, precise. Lean into the "Industrial Alpinism" as a sophisticated discipline, not just "cleaning windows."
- **Archetype**: The Sage/Explorer (technical mastery + overcoming vertical challenges).

## Tone
- **Professional & Expert**: Clear, direct, no fluff.
- **Safe & Reliable**: Emphasize documentation, certifications, and safety protocols.
- **Technologically Advanced**: Use terminology from engineering and modern construction.

## Anti-references
- Generic "cleaning services" websites with stock photos of smiling people.
- Overly playful or "friendly" branding (this is dangerous work; it needs gravitas).
- "SaaS-cream" aesthetics (rounded buttons, soft pastel shadows).
- AI-generated "futuristic" slop (fake glowing buildings).

## Strategic Principles
1. **Verifiable Trust**: Show real cases, real stats, real certifications.
2. **Precision over Polish**: Use technical drawings, blueprints, and data overlays rather than soft gradients.
3. **Verticality**: Use the layout to emphasize height and the "above-ground" nature of the work.
